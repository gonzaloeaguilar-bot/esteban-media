4. EXPLAINING THE 32% DIRECT CONVERSION RATE (168 Sessions -> 55 Conversions)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The fresh source shows:
  - `(direct) / (none)`: 168 sessions, 55 conversions (32.7% conversion rate).

This seemingly high conversion rate is explained by three verified factors:
  1. Multiple Key Events per Action (Dual-Emit Architecture):

       When a visitor taps WhatsApp or phone, `sendEvent` fires BOTH:

       click produces 2 conversion increments in GA4's aggregate `conversions` metric.
     - Thus, 55 conversions actually represent approximately 27-28 distinct contact actions.
  2. Qualified / High-Intent Direct Cohort & Retargeting:
     - Direct visitors include Esteban's direct prospect communications, returning
       clients, NFC business card taps, proposal review links, and WhatsApp chats
       navigating to the site.

       multiple times during their review of pricing and deliverables.

       2026-10-26) reveals concentrated multi-click bursts (e.g., 6 clicks on contact CTA
