# High-budget buyer path repair

Visitor: a South Florida marketing lead, agency, or event planner comparing production proposals.

Purpose: let that visitor understand what to decide before asking for a quote, inspect one published full-cycle assignment, and move into the existing RFP template without assuming a crew, schedule, or package.

The existing visual language stays intact. This is a short, server-rendered reading-path addition: answer-first procurement copy, one compact text link, and a published Healthy Smile case-study link. No animation, new control, or new lead form is introduced.

Acceptance: the new copy is present in the initial HTML, works at mobile and desktop widths without obscuring the existing contact action, preserves bilingual guide parity, and makes no unsupported capability, price, outcome, crew, or delivery claim.
