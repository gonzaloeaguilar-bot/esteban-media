# Pricing basis — budget estimator bands

**Last reviewed: 2026-08-12.**

Esteban does not yet have a confirmed internal price list. Until he does, the
budget estimator publishes **indicative ranges derived from South Florida market
rates for comparable work, less a standing 10% introductory discount**, and every
output is framed as an estimate requiring a scoped quote.

This file exists so the numbers are **sourced rather than invented**, which is what
`AGENTS.md` requires. Do not change a band without updating this file.

## The comparable that matters

Esteban's model is **editing-led**: remote post-production on client-supplied
footage, with selectively scoped on-location capture. The correct comparable is a
freelance editor / small studio.

It is **not** a Miami full-service production company. Those quote **$4,500–20,000**
for a corporate video because the figure assumes a full crew, shoot days, and
specialised equipment. Anyone benchmarking this estimator against that number will
conclude the bands are far too low; they are not, because it is the wrong model.

South Florida also carries a **0.80–0.90× cost multiplier against Los Angeles** as
the baseline market, so national rate guides are, if anything, slightly generous
for this area.

## Bands

Market band is the published 2026 range for comparable freelance work. Applied
band is that range narrowed to Esteban's typical scope, less 10%, rounded to $25.

| Service | Market band (2026) | Applied band | In market range? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Short-form social | $100–500 per project | **$350–675** | high end sits above the single-clip band; the market figure is per clip, this is a multi-deliverable project |
| YouTube edit | $300–1,500 | **$450–850** | yes |
| Corporate / explainer | $500–2,500 | **$725–1,450** | yes |
| Real-estate video | $250–1,200 | **$575–1,075** | yes |
| Ecommerce / product | $250–1,200 | **$400–775** | yes |
| On-location capture add-on | $300–1,000 half-day | **+$400–775** | yes |

Volume multipliers (5-video pack, 15/month, 30/month) and the 1.25× express
multiplier are unchanged — they describe scope, not market position.

## Rules this must keep satisfying

1. **Output stays a range, never a fixed quote.** The UI must continue to require a
   scoped quote before any commitment.
2. **No competitor's price list is copied.** Bands come from aggregate published
   rate guides, not from any single South Florida provider.
3. **The cost guides stay price-free.** `/guides/how-much-does-product-photography-cost`
   and its siblings deliberately assert no figure — they say there is no responsible
   one-price answer before scope is defined. That editorial stance is intentional and
   is not changed by this file.
4. **Replace this basis as soon as Esteban confirms real prices.** These are market
   proxies standing in for a price list that does not exist yet.
