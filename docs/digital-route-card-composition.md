# Digital route-card composition

The three digital cards on the homepage chooser should feel like clear, credible previews of work—not generic dark software tiles.

- **Websites and ecommerce:** show the real Gains From Geebs desktop hero large enough to recognise the person and the page in one glance.
- **AI agents and chatbots:** make the existing SNES-style Geebs companion the subject, with one concise assistant message. It can have a restrained idle breath, but brand logos and decorative dashboard clutter are out of scope.
- **SEO, GEO and landing pages:** reduce the scene to a readable search query and one destination result. The visual should explain the service immediately rather than simulate a product dashboard.

All three cards keep their current titles, body copy, native disclosure behaviour, analytics hooks, and existing route details. The visual is decorative; it never carries needed text or blocks the plus control. The Real Estate Brickell card and the approved Content Creator card are explicitly out of scope.
