# Storefront Monetization Audit — Esteban Moreno Media

Date: 2026-05-22

## Diagnosis

The site has a good technical SEO foundation: service pages, seven local/niche landing pages, bilingual EN/ES routes, sitemap coverage, LocalBusiness/Person/Service schema, and FAQ schema on local pages.

The weak point is monetization. Comparable storefronts make the buying path clearer with package anchors, visible proof, quote calls, booking CTAs, niche portfolios, and retainer offers. Esteban's site currently asks for an inquiry but does not yet package the decision for a buyer.

## Highest-Impact Changes

1. Add a `/packages` page with starting-at anchors.
2. Add three launch offers: edit-only starter, content day mini, and local business monthly.
3. Add package cards on the homepage, service pages, and local/niche pages.
4. Add a booking CTA: "Book a 15-minute content call" or "Send footage for a sample edit."
5. Expand the contact form with deadline, footage status, city, final platform, and shoot-needed fields.
6. Add portfolio slots by niche: Reels, real estate, restaurants, drone/aerial, events, product/business.
7. Add proof modules: testimonials, screenshots, before/after edits, client names, project outcomes.
8. Add a Google Business Profile launch checklist and match GBP services to site page language.
9. Add Search Console, Vercel Analytics, and conversion events after domain launch.
10. Build internal links from every package to the relevant local/niche page and contact form.

## Caveats

- Do not claim FAA Part 107, drone insurance, or controlled-airspace capability unless Esteban or a partner can prove it.
- Do not promise 24-hour, 48-hour, or same-week turnaround unless Esteban commits to the operating workflow.
- Do not add fake testimonials, client logos, awards, or review schema.
- Do not guarantee rankings.
- Public pricing should be framed as "starting at" or "typical range" until pricing is confirmed.
- Spanish copy should remain flagged for native review.
