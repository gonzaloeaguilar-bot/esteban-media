# Homepage work rail phone frame

## Brief

**Visitor and purpose.** A prospective video client scans the homepage work rail to judge Esteban's finished social-video work before opening a case.

**Change.** Keep the current card copy, destinations, horizontal project stills, and rail controls. For vertical Reel cards only, present the top media area inside a dark iPhone-like device frame with a small speaker/island detail. The frame belongs to the media preview, not to the whole card. Wide project posters remain fully visible inside their existing contained treatment, so text embedded in real work is never cropped.

**Mobile composition.** The device is centered with breathing room in the upper media area. Its video fills the screen and remains the card's link/tap surface. No new control, price, progress line, or mandatory motion is introduced. The frame remains readable without animation and does not affect reduced-motion behavior.

**Acceptance.** At 375x600, 390x844, and desktop, vertical Reel cards show a recognizable device frame without clipping the frame, hiding the project title, shrinking the video into a strip, or producing horizontal overflow. Wide still cards remain uncropped. Card links, keyboard focus, English/Spanish rendering, and reduced-motion behavior remain intact. Focused tests, lint, typecheck, build, browser screenshots, and an independent visual review are recorded before release.
