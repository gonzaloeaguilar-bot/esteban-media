EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & OPERATING PRINCIPLE
================================================================================
AI Answer Engines (ChatGPT/GPTBot, iAsk.ai, Duck.ai/Bing, Perplexity) do not evaluate
pages like traditional web indexes. They answer conversational questions by retrieving:
1. First-party entity authority (llms.txt, clean root NAP, transparent pricing, Schema.org).
2. Direct conversational question-and-answer headings (100-180 words, unmounted/native <details>).
3. Trusted 3rd-party aggregators and directories (Clutch, GigSalad, Collabstr, Zola, The Knot).
