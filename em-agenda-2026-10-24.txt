2. ROOT CAUSE OF THE GA4 CONVERSION DISCREPANCY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- In GA4, standard conversion tracking ("Key Events") requires events to be
  explicitly marked as Key Events in the GA4 Property Admin console.
- In Esteban's property (546162112), only legacy event names were registered as key
  events (or specifically `lead_submit`). If GA4 is only configured with
  `lead_submit` as a conversion/key event, then:
  * Direct phone calls (`contact_intent` / `contact_click`) are NOT counted as
    conversions in the GA4 conversions metric.
  * WhatsApp clicks (`contact_intent` / `contact_click`) are NOT counted as
    conversions in GA4.
  * Form submissions (`lead_submit` / `lead`) ARE counted, explaining why exactly
    1 conversion was recorded if only 1 contact form was submitted.
- Traffic quality / intent gap:
  * Over 450 programmatic SEO / service pages were launched across recent waves.
  * Informational / organic traffic may be landing on niche service pages where
    visitors read/consume without clicking WhatsApp or calling, or traffic is
    composed of desktop / research visits where mobile tap-to-call is less frequent.
